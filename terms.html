Terms of Service

Effective date: 2026-11-06

1. The service

Bookito is an app for recording expenses such as travel and living costs and, if you like, sharing them with the people you choose. The app does not send money or make payments on your behalf.

2. Your account

You sign up with an Apple or Google account. You are responsible for keeping your account information safe, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

3. Exchange rates and amounts

Exchange rates are European Central Bank (ECB) reference rates provided by Frankfurter. For each expense we use the latest daily rate published on or before the expense date, so weekends and holidays use the previous business day's rate. Of the 22 supported currencies, 16 get automatic rates and for the rest you enter the rate yourself. Exchange rates are for reference only and may differ from what your card is actually billed or what you actually exchange. If an amount calculated by the app differs from the amount actually paid, you can adjust the exchange rate or the billed amount yourself.

4. Your responsibilities

You must not use another person's information without permission, interfere with the operation of the service, or use it for any purpose that is against the law. Please keep in mind that what you enter in a shared book is visible to the people you share it with.

4-1. Content and reports

Names, memos, and other text you enter in a shared book must not be unlawful, harassing, or infringe others' rights. We may remove such content or suspend accounts that repeatedly break these rules. You can leave any shared book at any time, and you can report a problem or another member from Profile > Report a problem.

5. Changes to and suspension of the service

The service may change or be paused temporarily for improvements. We will try to announce important changes in the app. To guard against data loss, we recommend that you keep your records regularly using "Download my books and expenses (JSON)" in Profile.

5-1. Paid features and tips

Bookito is free to use. The free version may show a small banner ad. Ad removal and some features (groups of 3–6 people, recurring expenses in living books, card statement matching, CSV export, and recap cards) are offered as an optional paid upgrade called "Bookito Pro". You can choose a yearly subscription (auto-renewing) or a one-time lifetime purchase. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

On iPhone and iPad, payment is charged to your Apple ID and processed by the App Store. The yearly subscription renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings; refunds follow Apple's policy. If a subscription ends, your entries stay and you can still download them; only the Pro features lock again.

On Android, payment is charged to your Google Play account and processed by Google Play. The yearly subscription renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Google Play subscription settings; refunds follow Google Play's policy. Pro is tied to the store account you bought it with: Pro bought in one store (App Store or Google Play) may not yet be recognized in the other.

Tips are optional payments that support development and do not unlock anything.

The service is provided as is, and to the extent permitted by applicable law, liability for indirect and consequential damages is limited. This does not limit liability for our intentional misconduct or gross negligence.
