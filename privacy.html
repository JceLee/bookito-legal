Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-06

At a glance

Your records are stored on Supabase servers (US West) and are not used for advertising or analytics. The free version may show a Google AdMob banner ad.

People who join the same book can see your nickname and shared expenses.

You can delete your account at any time with Profile > Delete account in the app.

1. Information we collect

Sign-in: the unique identifier of your Apple or Google account, your email address (a hidden address if you use Apple's "Hide My Email"), and your name (used only as the default nickname the first time you sign in).

Profile: nickname, color, language, and time zone.

Service usage records: books, expenses (amount, currency, date, category, memo, payment method, share ratio), exchange rate settings, invitation records, and change history.

Paid features (Bookito Pro) and tips: Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes payments and we do not receive your card or payment details. We keep only whether Pro is active (its expiry time) on your device and do not send it to our servers.

If you turn on push notifications: your device's push token.

IOU notes (money owed to you or by you) are not sent to our servers; they stay on this device. They may be included in your device's own backup if you have that turned on, and they disappear from the device if you delete the app.

Some reminders, such as the weekly and monthly recaps, are created on your device and do not go through our servers.

Server operation logs: access times and request records (to keep the service stable).

Bookito does not collect location, contacts, or photos, and has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own. The banner ads in the free version are served by the Google AdMob ad SDK, with personalized ads turned off (only general ads are requested). To serve them, Google may collect and process ad and diagnostic data and device information (such as the device identifiers needed for ad requests, including the advertising ID on Android, approximate location and ad interactions); this information does not come to Bookito's servers. On Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. On iOS we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Where consent is required (for example in Europe) we show a consent form, and you can change your choice any time in Profile > Ad privacy choices. Ads are not shown when you use Bookito Pro.

2. How we use it

Identifying your account and keeping you signed in, saving your expense records and syncing them across devices and with the people you share with, calculating exchange rates, statistics, delivering notifications, responding to inquiries, and service security and error handling.

3. Information visible to others in a shared book

People who join the same book can see your nickname and shared expenses. Personal expenses you mark "Only me" are either not shown to the others at all (hidden), or are left out of totals only (visible personal expenses).

4. Retention and deletion

When you choose "Delete account" in the app, your profile is deleted, your personal expenses are deleted immediately, and books that only you use are permanently deleted after 30 days. Expenses you shared with others remain under the name "Former member" so that the other people's records stay intact, and so do settlement records and shared goals with their notes. When you leave a book, a read-only copy up to that point stays visible to you under "Books I left" for 90 days. The activity history of shared expenses (who added or changed what, with amount and category) is kept for up to 1 year, then deleted. Other activity records (such as book settings and membership changes) are kept as long as the book exists.

Expenses you delete can be restored from "Recently deleted" for 30 days, and are permanently deleted after that. Sync processing records (identifiers only, no content) are cleared after 60 days and the sync change log after 90 days; deletion markers are cleaned up after up to 1 year.

We take periodic backups of the database and keep them for a limited time (about 4 weeks). Information remaining in backups is removed as the backups expire.

5. Processors and international transfer

Data storage and authentication: Supabase Inc. — US West region (California). Kept from the time you sign in for as long as you use the service.

Sign-in providers: Apple Inc. and Google LLC (each company's own privacy policy applies).

Payment processing (Bookito Pro and tips): Apple Inc. (App Store) and Google LLC (Google Play) — each company processes payment details and we do not receive them.

Advertising (free version only; not shown with Pro): Google LLC (Google AdMob), in the United States and other countries. The device information needed to request an ad is passed to Google. Personalized ads are turned off and only general ads are requested; your expense records are never passed to the ad provider.

Exchange rates: Frankfurter (frankfurter.dev), which publishes European Central Bank (ECB) reference rates. Only our server requests rates; none of your personal information or expense details are sent.

Push notification delivery (only if you turn on push notifications): Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) — your push token and the notification text are passed to them. Reminders you set up on your device are scheduled on the device and are not sent to us.

Personal information may be transferred internationally to the companies above. If you do not want this, you can stop using the service by deleting your account.

6. Your rights

You can delete your account at any time with Profile > Delete account in the app, and you can download your books, expenses, and IOU notes with "Download my books and expenses (JSON)" in Profile. Please send requests to access, correct, or suspend the processing of your information to the contact below.

7. Children

Bookito is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not accept sign-ups from children under 14, or under the minimum age required by the law of their country if that is higher.
