Delete your account

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Bookito and go to My.
  2. Tap Delete account and read what will happen.
  3. Confirm with the same Apple or Google account, then type the confirmation word.

Your profile and personal expenses are deleted immediately. Books that only you use are permanently deleted after 30 days. Expenses you shared with others stay under the name “Former member” so the other people’s records stay intact. Backups expire within about 4 weeks.

If you cannot open the app

Email lkm4351@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (or tell us the Apple/Google account you used), and we will delete your account and data.
